# Phase 15 — Implementation result

## Outcome

Phase 15 installs the shared physical-Village facility foundation without enabling Restaurant or any other production facility economy. The existing Village art, five-tab navigation, passive Gold economy, Family assignments, First Covenant objective, roster progress, and save/recovery lineage remain in place.

## Delivered

- Twelve definition-backed Village hotspots with hidden, discovered, available, and ready presentation states.
- Facility-scoped canonical migration from the six broad predecessor anchors to the twelve authoritative physical anchors.
- One schema-12 Phase 15 activation receipt and exact successor validation; no schema increment or storage-key change.
- A single V2 claim authority for migrated Phase 12/13 pending offers, historical receipts, new Story/Legacy claims, and future facility claims.
- Immutable captured finalizer dispatch, manual non-expiring claims, source-typed receipt envelopes, a 512-receipt recent window, 128-receipt checkpoint folding, fixed V1 replay IDs, bounded facility ordinal ranges, and finite global source authority.
- Deterministic non-expiring opportunity settlement with checked safe-integer arithmetic, a QA-only 1,024 bank cap, and retained sub-interval carry when saturated.
- Five gradual, reward-neutral Phase 15 tutorials with distinct step copy, safe-surface delivery, Skip, Log, and Replay.
- All 38 shipped Fellows and Family remain registered for deterministic dialogue hooks; locked Fellows and unavailable art fall back safely without changing mechanical copy.
- Accessible facility sheet focus/return, Escape-neutral close, screen-reader names, 44×44 targets, reduced-motion behavior, mobile layouts, and no sixth navigation item.

## Migration and replay guarantees

Activation copies every predecessor pending offer and receipt into V2 before deleting the old `rewardClaims` field. Phase 12/13 offer creation, claim rows, and claim actions then use V2 adapters. Validation reconstructs a predecessor projection only on a clone, so no second live claim store exists.

V1 offer IDs remain in a fixed migrated replay set only when required. V2 facility history is represented permanently by compact claimed-ordinal ranges; Story and Legacy use the finite definition-derived global source set. Folded V2 receipts therefore do not grow an unbounded string ledger.
