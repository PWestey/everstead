# Prosperity and HQ decision record

## Locked now

- **Player Rank** governs Adventure access: routes, stages, systems, and progression milestones.
- **Prosperity** governs lifetime Village/HQ development only.
- Prosperity is non-spendable, never decreases, and is currently awarded by Oaths at Easy +2, Medium +4, and Hard +7.
- Prosperity does not currently multiply Gold or Power and cannot duplicate Player Rank gates.
- Buildings remain the source of spendable Gold.
