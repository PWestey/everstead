# Reference analysis and originality boundary

## How the references are used

The seven screenshots are usability references only. They are not project assets and may not be shipped, traced, sampled for icons, or reproduced as a screen composition. Their transferable value is limited to general principles such as hierarchy, progressive disclosure, large-art character identity, obvious readiness, and a single clear action.

Everstead keeps its own dark forest/obsidian surfaces, warm parchment text, teal interaction state, gold reward accents, restrained borders, Georgia display titles, five-tab information architecture, physical Village map, and accepted progression/economy.

The eighth image is different: it is user-supplied canonical Everstead protagonist art. Its exact bytes are preserved as the approved full-background `player.wayfarer` source.

## Originality checks before implementation acceptance

An independent reviewer compares each candidate surface side-by-side with the references and rejects:

- matching overall composition, panel proportions, decorative silhouette, or control placement;
- sampled or recreated icons, textures, borders, colors, fonts, labels, or button treatments;
- reference-specific terminology, currency structure, task cadence, roster staffing, or progression;
- a screen that would be recognizable as a reskin rather than an Everstead surface.

Similarity at the level of ordinary UI conventions—tabs, cards, progress bars, a portrait backdrop, a list, or a primary button—is acceptable only when expressed through Everstead's established system and information architecture.
