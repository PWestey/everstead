## Coverage gate

Before the Phase 21 release is considered content-complete:

- every one of the 18 Fellow IDs and 20 Family IDs in `cast-plan.json` must have at least one shipped profile quote, one eligible ambient line, and one authored dialogue appearance;
- a Fellow's live appearance must respect the Phase 11G join Rank;
- no scene should add a cast member merely to satisfy the ledger—their assigned function must matter to that interaction;
- content can be registered before speaker art is ready, but a Village scene can visually render that speaker only through an approved transparent cutout or intentionally approved framed treatment;
- the original full-background character-sheet asset is preserved and may not be reused as an unframed Village dialogue overlay.
